## What the game already has (seen in code)

3D one-finger cut-the-rope puzzle for ages 5–11: each of 50 levels (10 worlds of 5: living room, bathroom, garden,
kitchen, beach, party, farm, snow, space, toy shop) hangs three or four things on ropes; one cut fixes the scene,
others end in a harmless funny mess; every ending is a sticker (168 in a sticker book); balloons and cut order in
later worlds; level map; stars; 8 languages; sound made in code; haptics; saves; leaderboard of stars plus stickers;
adaptive quality. Same puzzle family as Rub It Away.
