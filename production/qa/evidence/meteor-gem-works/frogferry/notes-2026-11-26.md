# Meteor Gems: lessons from the Frog Ferry study (2026-11-26)

- **Personal bests.** Three are tracked: gems made, a perfect-strike streak, and coins in the best rolling minute.
  - All three are saved (`best` in the save). The bests from the previous session are the bar for this one.
  - A stat that beats its bar raises one "NEW RECORD!" beat per session, with a fanfare, a camera beat and the share offer (`ff-2-new-record.jpg`).
  - A first session stays quiet: the bar must be at least `records.min`.
- **Colour language** (`kindColor`): chunk violet, gem teal (the stand awning teal), ring gold.
  - The carry label gets a dot in that colour.
  - The order bubble gets a rim in that colour.
  - The sale "+N" pop uses that colour.
  - The game has one gem type, not one per colour, so there is no per-colour stand to match.
- **Cheer toy.** It is the third button in the right column, on the same 8 px grid.
  - The hero waves, the crowd does a wave, visitors and critters hop, and a few sparkles pop.
  - Its chord climbs with each press. It has no score effect (`ff-3-cheer-toy.jpg`).
- **Tilt-shift.** A quarter-size blurred copy of the frame is mixed in outside a soft band at the hero, or at the subject during a beat.
  - It runs on tier 2+ (the post-processing tiers) only, and is off on tier 0.
  - Cost: two extra passes at 1/16 of the pixels, plus one texture read per pixel.
  - On SwiftShader the frame time with and without it is within noise (about 1%). A real phone is not measured yet.
- **Share text** carries the best gems stat ("I made 45 gems in Meteor Gems!").
- **Loader.** It is already plain HTML/CSS: `#app:not(.live)` paints a sky and a glowing meteor before any script runs. No change was needed.
