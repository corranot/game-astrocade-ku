# Snip It: Cut to Fix — brief

**Question:** does a ten-second, one-swipe "cut the right rope" stage with funny wrong answers hold a child past 40 seconds?
**Date:** 2026-11-24

- **Player:** children 5-11, casual players in any of 8 languages; portrait phone, one finger.
- **Core loop:** look at the stage (3 ropes) → swipe one → watch the ending (about 2 s) → sticker → next or try the other ropes.
- **Why it could work:** the one-finger puzzle family with funny reactions is the only one of ours that passed the first-200-plays gate this month; cutting is a new verb in it.
- **Rules:** one right rope (3 stars, opens the next level), two wrong ropes (a harmless gag each). No lives, no timer, no fail screen. 3 stickers per level.
- **Look:** bright toy-box dioramas in 3D; nothing borrowed from other rope games (no candy, no monster).
- **Scope:** 12 levels at launch; more through updates.
- **Risks:** our last three 3D games stalled under 120 plays; FPS on weak phones is unproven; 12 levels is about 2-3 minutes of play.
- **Done when:** median session of 40 s or more on the first 200 plays, FPS "Optimal" in the creator analytics.
