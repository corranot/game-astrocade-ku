Nearest game: Kiki's Doll Hair Salon (Astrocade, Olliee); this one differs in: real simulated strands that are cut where the finger crosses them and fall, an order card scored in a mirror reveal with 1-3 stars, five different gestures in a fixed recipe (rub, cut along a moving dotted line, paint, hold to dry, tap to decorate), animal customers, and a salon wall of the player's own photos.
